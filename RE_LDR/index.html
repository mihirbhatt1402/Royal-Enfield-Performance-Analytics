⚠ W3 E2B Collapse — Booking Rate Hits 0.38%
W3 bookings (88) fell 52% from W2 (184) despite leads growing 24% to 23,352. E2B dropped from 0.98% to 0.38% — the lowest rate across all three weeks. Spend rose 62% (₹3.86L → ₹6.27L), suggesting the algorithm is still in a learning phase as state-model audience buckets continue to scale. Immediate review of audience targeting and creative refresh warranted.
✅ CPL Improving — Algorithm Learning Paying Off
Despite lower E2B, CPL efficiency improved in W3: MS FB CPL fell from ₹57 to ₹47 (▼18%) and WhatsApp CPL fell from ₹52 to ₹37 (▼30%). Lead volume scaled strongly — WhatsApp unique leads jumped 38% (7,640 → 10,533) and MS FB grew 53% (3,368 → 5,155). The funnel top is filling; conversion intent is the bottleneck.
🔍 Adwords Scaling Well — E2B Holding at 0.77%
Adwords unique leads grew 38% (564 → 776) with 6 bookings at 0.77% E2B. Hunter 350 (339 leads) and Bullet 350 (159) dominate the search mix in W3. Search channel maintains meaningful conversion relative to Meta despite the scale-up pressure on overall E2B.

🔍 Search Intent = Higher Booking Intent
Adwords grew to 776 unique leads in W3 (from 568 in W2), a 37% jump, but E2B dropped to 0.77% (6 bookings) vs 2.64% in W2. Dup rate edged up to 37.9%, suggesting retargeting overlap is growing. Despite lower E2B, volume is scaling — W3 spend yielded strong cost-per-unique metrics as quality search traffic builds. Bullet 350 and Hunter 350 drove the lead volume; Interceptor 650 and Guerrilla 450 showed strong W2→W3 growth signals.

Adwords Bookings Breakdown (W2 → W3)
W2: 15 bookings across Hunter 350 (4), Bullet 350 (4), Classic 350 (4), Meteor 350 (3) — strong 2.64% E2B. W3: 6 bookings across Hunter 350 (4), Bullet 350 (1), Classic 350 (1) — lower absolute conversion but volume grew 37%. The E2B pullback from 2.64% to 0.77% in W3 suggests the search funnel expanded into earlier-stage users. Continue optimising for high-intent keywords; Meteor 350 search terms merit revisit given its strong W2 performance.
Watch: Duplicate Rate at 35%
Adwords dup rate (35%) is the highest among paid channels. This suggests a significant share of searchers are already in the RE CRM (existing enquirers, past leads). Consider negative audience lists from existing CRM segments to avoid burning budget on already-known prospects — or route them directly to a DMS dealer callback instead of a standard lead form.
Pan India → State-wise: Lead Distribution Reshuffled
The Meta restructure shifted MS FB leads out of high-volume states (Gujarat –55%, Rajasthan –54%, Maharashtra –76%) while WhatsApp expanded aggressively into the same states (+59–85%). Net result: overall unique leads declined in Gujarat and Rajasthan but grew in UP and Karnataka, and the channel mix within each state changed significantly.
