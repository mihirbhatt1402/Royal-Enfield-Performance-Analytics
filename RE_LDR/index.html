⚠ W2 Booking Decline Confirmed — Not a Lag Artefact
Booking data is now complete. W2 bookings (191) are lower than W1 (231) — a real 17% decline, not a reporting lag. The structural drop across channels is confirmed: E2B fell from 1.96% to 1.56% despite higher WhatsApp spend. Priority should be diagnosing lower conversion intent from the new state-model audience buckets.
⚡ Spend 2x'd, Efficiency Halved — The Restructure Cost
Total Meta spend jumped 94% (₹1.99L → ₹3.86L), driven almost entirely by WhatsApp budgets scaling 4x. Yet combined paid CPL nearly doubled — MS FB from ₹33 to ₹57 (+73%) and WhatsApp from ₹22 to ₹52 (+136%). The state-wise targeting shift means campaigns are in a learning phase; efficiency should recover over W3–W4 as the algorithm optimises within state-model audience buckets.
✅ Adwords Hit the Ground Running
The Search campaign launched with 568 unique leads in its first full week and delivered an E2B of 2.64% — the best among all paid channels, beating MS FB (1.29%) and WhatsApp (1.35%). Hunter 350 leads the Adwords mix (235 unique leads), followed by Bullet 350 (154) and Classic 350 (89). This channel validates the demand signal for high-intent search traffic.

🔍 Search Intent = Higher Booking Intent
Adwords' 2.64% E2B is 2x MS FB's (1.29%) and nearly 2x WhatsApp's (1.35%) in W2 — confirming that people searching for Royal Enfield models are far closer to purchase. The 35% dup rate (vs 28.2% for MS FB) suggests some searchers are already in the CRM, but the unique pipeline of 568 is strong for a first full week.

Adwords Bookings Breakdown (W2)
15 bookings across 4 models: Hunter 350 (4), Bullet 350 (4), Classic 350 (4), Meteor 350 (3). Strong conversion across all 4 top models validates broad search demand. Meteor 350's 3 bookings from 23 unique leads (13% E2B) warrants increased search budget allocation toward Meteor 350 search terms in W3.
Watch: Duplicate Rate at 35%
Adwords dup rate (35%) is the highest among paid channels. This suggests a significant share of searchers are already in the RE CRM (existing enquirers, past leads). Consider negative audience lists from existing CRM segments to avoid burning budget on already-known prospects — or route them directly to a DMS dealer callback instead of a standard lead form.
Pan India → State-wise: Lead Distribution Reshuffled
The Meta restructure shifted MS FB leads out of high-volume states (Gujarat –55%, Rajasthan –54%, Maharashtra –76%) while WhatsApp expanded aggressively into the same states (+59–85%). Net result: overall unique leads declined in Gujarat and Rajasthan but grew in UP and Karnataka, and the channel mix within each state changed significantly.
